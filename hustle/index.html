THE HUSTLE FORMULA

A little absurd.
Actually useful.

We don't start with technology. We start with the moment you sigh, “Ugh, not again.” Then we imagine a ridiculous shortcut—and engineer backwards to the cheapest sensible test.

Our north star is that split second where you laugh at the idea... and immediately want one.

01 / NOTICE

The annoying moment

Find a small, frequent, emotionally relatable friction someone has accepted as normal.

02 / TWIST

The impossible-looking trick

Borrow a mechanism from somewhere unexpected: retractors, magnets, rails, gravity, tags, soft robotics.

03 / PROVE

The cheap, safe test

Validate desire and utility, prototype at low energy and small scale, then check engineering and commercial reality.
